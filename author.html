Oliver Bennett

Senior Culinary Journalist & Asian Dining Specialist

Oliver Bennett has spent over 12 years covering the UK restaurant sector, with a primary focus on Japanese fusion street food, high-street casual dining, and dietary menu evolution. Based in Soho, London, Oliver regularly visits kitchens and reviews new menu launches across England, Scotland, and Wales.

Background & Culinary Credentials

Holding a BA in English Literature from University College London (UCL) and a Diploma in Food Journalism, Oliver began his writing career covering London's street food scene and borough markets. He later specialized in analyzing high-street chain menus, focusing on price transparency, nutritional accountability, and plant-based innovation.

Areas of Expertise

  • Pan-Asian & Japanese Fusion: Deep understanding of broth preparation, noodle varieties, and Japanese curry profiles.
  • Nutritional Analysis: Evaluating sodium levels, macro balances, and allergen matrices for British diners.
  • UK Casual Dining Market: Tracking pricing models and menu trends across major UK urban centers.
