Deep-Dive Wagamama Katsu Curry Guide: Calories, Sauce & Plant-Based Options

The story, sauce secrets, nutritional facts, and complete variations of the dish that redefined British high-street Japanese cuisine.

Written by Oliver Bennett | Senior UK Culinary Journalist

If there is one dish that has attained legendary status among UK diners over the past three decades, it is undoubtedly Japanese Katsu Curry. Combining golden crispy panko-coated cutlets with a velvety, comforting curry sauce and sticky white rice, this signature menu creation accounts for millions of orders annually across Great Britain.

In this guide, we analyze why the Katsu Curry became a cultural phenomenon, break down the nutritional facts for each variation, and explore how plant-based innovations expanded its appeal to millions of vegan and vegetarian foodies.

The Anatomy of the Perfect Katsu Curry

What makes a great Katsu Curry? The classic version balances three distinct elements on the plate:

  • The Cutlet (Katsu): Tender chicken breast coated in light Japanese panko breadcrumbs, fried until golden and crisp.
  • The Curry Sauce: A sweet, mild, and savory sauce crafted from a blend of onions, garlic, ginger, coconut milk, soy sauce, garamu masala, and turmeric.
  • The Accompaniments: A generous dome of sticky white rice served alongside a fresh side salad dressed with a tangy shallot dressing and pickled beni shoga ginger.

Katsu Curry Variations Available in the UK

Diners can choose between several exciting spins on the classic original dish:

  1. Chicken Katsu Curry (£15.20): The original icon. 1,085 kcal per serving.
  2. Hot Chicken Katsu Curry (£15.50): Infused with extra hot red chilies and chili oil for spicy food enthusiasts.
  3. Vegatsu (£14.95): Made with crispy panko-crusted seitan cutlets, providing an identical texture to traditional chicken (£14.95).
  4. Yasai Katsu Curry (£14.50): Featuring panko-fried sweet potato, aubergine, and butternut squash slices.

Pro Ordering Tips for Katsu Lovers

Want to elevate your dining experience? Here are top tips from UK food editor Oliver Bennett:

  • Ask for Extra Sauce: Did you know you can request an additional small bowl of hot curry sauce free of charge or for a nominal fee? It guarantees your last bite of rice is just as flavor-packed as the first.
  • Swap Rice for Brown Rice: Guests looking for higher dietary fiber can request a brown rice substitution.
  • Try National Katsu Curry Day Specials: Keep an eye on October menu launches when limited-edition Katsu variations, such as Katsu Ya (curry pie hybrids) or Giant Katsu platters, appear in select UK locations.
