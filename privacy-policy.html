Privacy Policy & Cookie Notice

How wagamamenue protects reader privacy under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Overview & Data Controller

At wagamamenue, we respect the privacy of our readers across the United Kingdom and worldwide. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected when you browse our website, how that data is used, and your statutory rights under UK law.

The Data Controller for this website is wagamamenue Editorial Desk, located in London, United Kingdom. If you have questions regarding data privacy, contact us at privacy@wagamama-menu.netlify.app.

2. Information We Collect

We minimize data collection to what is strictly necessary to deliver a fast, secure website experience:

  • Non-Personal Technical Logs: Standard web server log files record IP addresses, browser user agents, referring pages, and access timestamps to ensure site security and prevent malicious traffic.
  • Contact Form Data: When you submit a contact inquiry, we receive your name, email address, and message text solely to respond to your request.
  • Anonymized Analytics: Aggregated, privacy-focused traffic stats help us understand which culinary guides are most useful to UK readers. No persistent tracking profiles are created.

3. Cookie Usage Policy

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. wagamamenue uses essential functional cookies to deliver basic site performance. We do not use intrusive third-party cross-site tracking cookies or sell personal data to advertising networks.

4. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

As a UK resident, you hold statutory data protection rights, including:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Erasure ('Right to be Forgotten'): Request deletion of your personal data from our contact records.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request that we limit processing of your data under specific circumstances.

5. External Links Disclaimer

Our articles may contain links to external third-party websites (such as official restaurant portals). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external third-party domains. We encourage users to review the privacy notices of any linked external site.
